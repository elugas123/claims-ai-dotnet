# Contoso Insurance — Auto Comprehensive Policy (AUTO-COMP-2026)

> Synthetic document for a fictional insurer. Not a real insurance contract.

## 1. Covered events

1.1 **Collision.** Damage to the insured vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle, a fixed object, or by overturning.

1.2 **Theft.** Total theft of the insured vehicle, or partial theft of parts permanently attached to it. A police report must be filed within 48 hours of discovering the theft.

1.3 **Glass.** Repair or replacement of windshield, windows and mirrors, with no deductible.

1.4 **Natural events.** Hail, flood and falling trees.

1.5 **Third-party liability.** Bodily injury and property damage caused to third parties by the insured driver, up to USD 500,000 per event.

## 2. Exclusions

2.1 Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

2.2 Drivers without a valid license for the vehicle class.

2.3 Use of the vehicle for ride-hailing, delivery or any commercial purpose, unless the "Commercial Use" rider is active.

2.4 Racing, speed tests or off-road competitions.

2.5 Mechanical failure, wear and tear, or lack of maintenance.

2.6 Personal belongings left inside the vehicle.

## 3. Deductibles

3.1 Collision: USD 500 per event.

3.2 Theft: 10% of the vehicle's insured value.

3.3 Glass: no deductible.

## 4. Reporting obligations

4.1 The policyholder must report the claim within **72 hours** of the incident.

4.2 The report must include: date, time and location of the incident; policy number; vehicle plate; description of what happened; and, if a third party is involved, their name, plate and insurer.

4.3 Collision claims above USD 3,000 require photos of the damage and a repair estimate from an approved workshop.

4.4 Any claim involving injuries or theft requires a police report number.
