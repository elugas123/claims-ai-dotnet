# Contoso Insurance — Home Standard Policy (HOME-STD-2026)

> Synthetic document for a fictional insurer. Not a real insurance contract.

## 1. Covered events

1.1 **Fire.** Damage to the building and contents caused by fire, lightning or explosion.

1.2 **Water damage.** Sudden and accidental escape of water from pipes, tanks or appliances.

1.3 **Burglary.** Theft of contents with signs of forced entry. A police report must be filed within 24 hours.

1.4 **Storm.** Damage caused by wind, hail or the weight of snow.

## 2. Exclusions

2.1 Gradual leaks, humidity, mold or damage that developed over time.

2.2 Damage while the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days.

2.3 Theft without signs of forced entry.

2.4 Cash, jewelry and artwork above USD 2,000 per item unless individually declared.

2.5 Intentional damage caused by the policyholder or residents.

## 3. Deductibles

3.1 Fire and storm: USD 250 per event.

3.2 Water damage: USD 300 per event.

3.3 Burglary: USD 200 per event.

## 4. Reporting obligations

4.1 The policyholder must report the claim within **7 days** of the incident.

4.2 The report must include: date and address of the incident; policy number; description of the damage; and a list of damaged or stolen items with estimated values.

4.3 Burglary claims require a police report number.

4.4 Water damage claims require a plumber's report identifying the source of the leak.
